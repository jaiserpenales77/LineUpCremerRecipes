How to use

  1. 🔎
    1. Find a recipe

    Type the FG or bulk number in the search box, e.g. 2684 or A641. Or tap a line or bottle size to narrow the list.

  2. 👆
    2. Open it

    Tap a recipe card. The four big numbers at the top are the main settings; everything else is below.

  3. 🖨️
    3. Print it

    Tap Print for a one-page sheet. For colour, turn on Background graphics in the print settings. Need it in Excel? Tap Download Excel version under the title: a copy with the same search and a printable recipe card.

  4. 💡
    4. Have better settings?

    Tap Have better settings?, change the numbers, add your name and shift, tap Print and give the sheet to your supervisor. Don’t change the machine until it’s approved.

  5. 🕘
    5. What changed?

    The History tab on the right edge shows which recipes changed and when. Tap it to open or close.

Supervisors only
  1. 🔒
    6. Manage

    Unlocked with the Super password, the Manage tab lets you Edit, add (+ New recipe) or Remove recipes. Changes save for everyone straight away and show in the update history.

✅ Always check the settings on the machine before running.
